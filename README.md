Build a beautiful, modern, premium single-page website for COAST CANE®, a cold-pressed sugarcane juice brand.

The website should feel like a major modern tropical beverage brand while remaining warm, approachable, natural, youthful and authentic to the existing Coast Cane identity.

IMPORTANT:
Do not make this look like a generic restaurant website.
Do not make it look like a corporate juice company.
Do not completely change the existing Coast Cane branding.

Instead, take the existing visual identity and elevate it into a polished, modern digital brand experience.

FINAL DESIGN TEST
==================================================

Before considering the website complete, ask:

Does this feel like a modern tropical beverage brand?

Does it immediately communicate fresh sugarcane juice?

Does the Coast Cane identity remain recognizable?

Does the website look significantly more premium than the current social media graphics without losing their personality?

Can someone visiting from Instagram understand the business within 5 seconds?

Can they place an order within 10 seconds?

Does the website look excellent on a mobile phone?

Is the design visually exciting without being cluttered?

If the answer to any of these is no, refine the design.

The final result should feel like a real, professionally designed beverage brand website — not an AI-generated template.
